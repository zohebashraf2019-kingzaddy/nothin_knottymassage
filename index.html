FAQ

Questions? I've got you.

How do I book an appointment?
Use the Book Now page to plan your service, length, add-ons, date and time, then confirm on my secure online booking page. You can also call or text (775) 433-8937 or DM @nothin_knottymassage on Instagram.
Do you offer house calls / mobile massage?
Yes! I can bring the massage table and everything you need right to your home. Mobile massage is an additional $30 on top of the service price.
What should I do before my first appointment?
Please arrive at least 10 minutes early to fill out a short intake form so I can learn about any areas of concern, injuries or preferences. Wear something comfortable, stay hydrated and avoid a heavy meal right before your session.
What is your cancellation policy?
Please cancel at least 4 hours before your appointment time to avoid fees. Late cancellations are charged 40% of the service price, and no-call/no-shows are charged the full service price. Thank you for understanding!
Which massage is right for me?
Swedish is perfect for general relaxation. Aromatherapy adds therapeutic essential oils for stress relief. Hot Stone uses warmth to melt tension, and Deep Tissue Hot Stone targets chronic tightness in deeper muscle layers. Not sure? Book what feels closest and we'll tailor the session together.
Do you offer prenatal massage?
Yes — my prenatal massage is gentle and nurturing, designed to ease common pregnancy discomforts, and includes a soothing belly massage with Vitamin E oils to hydrate the skin and support stretch mark prevention. Please check with your healthcare provider if you have any concerns about massage during pregnancy.
How do I choose an aromatherapy oil?
Check out the aromatherapy guide above — lavender for stress, peppermint for tension relief, eucalyptus for easier breathing and more. You can add aromatherapy to any massage for $25, or let me help you pick when you arrive.
Can I pay over time?
Yes — services $100 and up can be paid with Affirm, Klarna or Afterpay at checkout on the booking page, as low as about $9/month or interest-free.
¿Hablas español?
¡Sí! I'm happy to welcome both English- and Spanish-speaking clients. Feel free to message me in either language.
How do I enter the "Year of Free Massages" giveaway?
Book and receive any massage service between November 1 and December 31, 2026 and you're automatically entered. The raffle is December 31. The prize is one 60-minute Swedish massage per month for all of 2027 — non-transferable, in-studio only (excludes house calls).
