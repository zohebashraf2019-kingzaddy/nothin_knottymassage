Hi, my name is Juliet!

I'm so excited to welcome you to my massage business, Nothin' Knotty Massage, here in Reno, Nevada.

I became a massage therapist because I have a genuine passion for helping people feel relaxed, relieve pain and muscle tension, and improve their overall well-being. I believe massage is more than just a moment of relaxation — it's an opportunity to slow down, reconnect with yourself, and give your body the care it deserves.

I'm so excited to bring my passion to life in my very own massage space, where I can provide a peaceful and welcoming environment for my clients. I also offer mobile massage services, bringing relaxation and relief right to the comfort of your home.

As a Spanish speaker, I'm happy to welcome both English- and Spanish-speaking clients! My goal is to make every client feel comfortable, cared for, and refreshed — whether you're coming in to unwind, ease muscle tension, or simply take some much-needed time for yourself.
